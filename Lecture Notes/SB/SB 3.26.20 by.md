# Text
> viśvam ātma-gataṁ vyañjan
> kūṭa-stho jagad-aṅkuraḥ
> sva-tejasāpibat tīvram
> ātma-prasvāpanaṁ tamaḥ

# Translation
> Thus, after manifesting variegatedness, the effulgent mahat-tattva, which contains all the universes within itself, which is the root of all cosmic manifestations and which is not destroyed at the time of annihilation, swallows the darkness that covered the effulgence at the time of dissolution.

# Key Points

- **Jagat** got **ankurita** from [[Mahat-Tattva]]. 
- A scientist can't come up with some thing new. It can be just that something was initially dormant and now it manifested. 
- Our contemplation or [[thinking]] of any idea is the seed. So repeated [[thinking]] will plant the seed. And then it will grow out. 
- [[BG 18.18]]: two kinds of work ([[subtle]] and [[gross]]) for anything to manifest. 
- Thinking of a mature devotee: Everything is part of [[Krsna]]'s plan. Whatever one gets in his hand is due to the sanction of [[God]]. 
- While doing #karma_sangraha aspect we should not focus on the homework or #karma_codana aspects. 
- [[BG 4.34]]: blank slate. Status of current generation - they have access to all the information like various BG, SB lectures by various eloquent speakers, various reading materials, notes, purports, commentaries, etc. But nevertheless the current generation has no [[realization]], no [[Sadacara]]. 
- Simply knowing something is not what we want. We should have [[realization]], we should feelings or emotions. It should not be shallow. 
- We should not be a better [[Paramahamsa]] compared to [[Prabhupada]]. 
- The significance of #Mindfulness was hilighted. If we have to listen SB, then do that. Not sleep or reply to msgs. If you have to chant, then only japa, nothing else should be done.
