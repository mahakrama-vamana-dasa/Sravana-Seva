# Text
> ṛṣir uvāca
> ity avyalīkaṁ praṇuto ’bja-nābhas
> tam ābabhāṣe vacasāmṛtena
> suparṇa-pakṣopari rocamānaḥ
> prema-smitodvīkṣaṇa-vibhramad-bhrūḥ

# Translation
> Maitreya resumed: Sincerely extolled in these words, Lord Visnu, shining very beautifully on the shoulders of Garuḍa, replied with words as sweet as nectar. His eyebrows moved gracefully as He looked at the sage with a smile full of affection.

# Key Points

- [[Kardama]] glorified [[Lord Visnu]] and thus He poured nectar unto [[Kardama]]. 
- Whatever [[God|Lord]] does is [[Transcendental]]. 
- Because we are not #hearing the [[Nama japa]] thus we become #exhausted. We are hearing something else. 
- Because [[Kardama]] prayed #sincerely thus, [[Lord Visnu]] reciprocated #immediately. Similarly, if we want quick immediate reciprocation, then we should pray #sincerely. 
- Some parts from the purport of #Hare_Krsna_Mahamantra was quoted.
