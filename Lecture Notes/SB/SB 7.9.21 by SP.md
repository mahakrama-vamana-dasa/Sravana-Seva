# Text
> māyā manaḥ sṛjati karmamayaṁ balīyaḥ
> kālena codita-guṇānumatena puṁsaḥ
> chandomayaṁ yad ajayārpita-ṣoḍaśāraṁ
> saṁsāra-cakram aja ko ’titaret tvad-anyaḥ

# Translation
> O Lord, O supreme eternal, by expanding Your plenary portion You have created the subtle bodies of the living entities through the agency of Your external energy, which is agitated by time. Thus the mind entraps the living entity in unlimited varieties of desires to be fulfilled by the Vedic directions of karma-kāṇḍa \[fruitive activity] and the sixteen elements. Who can get free from this entanglement unless he takes shelter at Your lotus feet?

# Key Points

- `sarvam khalv idam brahma`
- [[BG 9.4]]: `maya tatam idam sarvam`, [[Krsna]] is spread all over the [[Material world|existence]] but we can't see Him for He is in the avyakta murti. 
- [[BG 7.4]]: 8 separated [[External energy|energies]] of [[Krsna]]
- [[BS 5.1]]: isvarah paramah krsnah 
- There is nothing other than [[Krsna]]. 
- [[BG 9.10]]: don't be rascal to think that accidents take place daily. Hyperfinetunning of various things in nature proves the presence of [[God]]. 
- Not a blade of grass moves without the desire of [[Krsna]]. 
- [[BS 5.44]]: srsti sthiti pralaya sadhana sakti durga
- [[Durga]]: duh (indicates negatives like hard or difficult as we use in duh mati, duh gati, duh svapna, etc.) + ga (similar to gati, gata, etc.)
