# Text
> tasmād amūs tanu-bhṛtām aham āśiṣo ’jña  
> āyuḥ śriyaṁ vibhavam aindriyam āviriñcyāt  
> necchāmi te vilulitān uruvikrameṇa  
> kālātmanopanaya māṁ nija-bhṛtya-pārśvam

# Translation
> My dear Lord, now I have complete experience concerning the worldly opulence, mystic power, longevity and other material pleasures enjoyed by all living entities, from Lord Brahmā down to the ant. As powerful time, You destroy them all. Therefore, because of my experience, I do not wish to possess them. My dear Lord, I request You to place me in touch with Your pure devotee and let me serve him as a sincere servant.

# Key Points

- `āyuḥ`: karmis want to increase their age. They don't want to die. People spend so much money for operations, etc. to keep oneself young. 
- `āviriñcyāt`: even **virinci** is in the [[Material world]]. Why? To become the master of this [[Material world]]. 
- Our real [[Disease]] is how to become master. [[KC]] means to giveup the nonsense idea of becoming [[master]]. 
- Different [[Jiva]]s have different degrees of bhoga vancha desires. Someone want to just enjoy the family, someone entire [[Material world]]. 
- [[SB 7.9.21]] or `maya manah` describes [[Material Existence]]. 
- [[BS 5.34]]: mental speculators or muni-pungu can't approach [[God]] even at the speed of [[Mind]] or air. 
- [[BG 18.66]]: you rascal give up all these socalled engagement and surrender unto Me. Then you will be free from [[Maya]]. Same is there in [[BG 7.14]]. 
- [[Lord Caitanya]] starts His teachings from the point of [[BG 18.66]]. 
- [[Sanatana Gosvami]]'s pastime of approaching [[Lord Caitanya]] and asking "who am i? why am i suffering?" 
- [[Madhya 20.108]] or `jivera svarupa haya nitya krsna dasa`
- If a servant is imitating the master in an office, then he will suffer. 
- [[Supreme|Absolute]] Truth is sentient, svarat, independent. He is nija labha purna 
- [[Maya]] means we want to imitate [[Krsna]]. 
- When a [[Mayavadi]] thinks that he is [[God]] then he is in the darkest region of [[Maya]]. 
- The beginning or cause of [[Material Existence]] 
- [[BG 7.19]]: bahunam janmanam ante
- [[BG 7.15]]: though they have [[jnana]] but it is stolen by [[Maya]]. 
- [[BS 5.44]]: the function of [[Durga]] was discussed. 
- United Nation, etc. not possible. Currently, there is atheistic, demonic civilization. 
- Vedic culture is the best culture.

- [[Spiritual perfection]] is to become servant of servant. Trnadapi sunicena: a [[servant]] has to tolerate.
