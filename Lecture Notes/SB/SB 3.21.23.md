# Text
> śrī-bhagavān uvāca
> viditvā tava caityaṁ me
> puraiva samayoji tat
> yad-artham ātma-niyamais
> tvayaivāhaṁ samarcitaḥ

# Translation
> The Supreme Lord said: Having come to know what was in your mind, I have already arranged for that for which you have worshiped Me well through your mental and sensory discipline.

Short summary of the main theme of the lecture.

# Key Points

- [[BG 15.15]]: [[Paramatma]] sits in our heart. He knows everything in regard to our desire, mental condition or `caityam`. 
- `atma niyama` or discipline of [[Mind]] and [[Sense(s)]] is required. 
- [[Sad Vidha Saranagati]]: we need to reject desires against [[Devotional service]] and accept desires favorable for [[Devotional service]]. 
- [[BG 9.22]]: ananyas cintayanto mam
- Our [[Desire]] should align with [[Desire]]s of [[Guru]] and [[Krsna]]. We should not [[Desire]] whimsically or independently.  
- A father will share the password or key of home locker to the faithful son who will not waste the money. 
- [[BG 13.23]]: we are also [[Purusa]] or enjoyer but we are not para-purusa or transcendenal [[Purusa]]. 
	- Even for our [[Sense Gratification]], He is the sanctioner and facilitators. 
	- We should not forget the underlying principle of [[Purification of heart]]. 
- [[BG 3.27]]: 
- `samayoji`: [[Lord Visnu]] had already arranged what [[Kardama]] wanted. Similarly, if we want to build a temple for [[Prabhupada]] or for that matter follow any instruction of [[Prabhupada]], then everything will be arranged. 
- [[Prabhupada]] desired what his [[Guru]] desired. So, we should adopt the [[Desire]] of [[Parampara]]. 
- Indra and Prthu's pastime of 100th yajna was narrated. 
- [[SB 4.20]]: `Lord Viṣṇu’s Appearance in the Sacrificial Arena of Mahārāja Pṛthu`
- We should count the fortune of having [[Prabhupada]] as the external manifestation of [[Paramatma]] or caittya guru. 
- Lip service will not help. We should be really determined to [[Back to Godhead]] in this life. [[Krsna]] sees our [[Determination]].
