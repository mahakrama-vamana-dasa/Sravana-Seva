# Text

> sudhiyaḥ sādhavo loke
> naradeva narottamāḥ
> nābhidruhyanti bhūtebhyo
> yarhi nātmā kalevaram

# Translation
> O King, one who is advanced in intelligence and eager to perform welfare activities for others is considered best amongst human beings. An advanced human being is never malicious to others. Those with advanced intelligence are always conscious that this material body is different from the soul.

# Analogies / Examples

- **Watching movie**: 
## Car Race Video Game
We can choose our car, color, model, etc. We are outside the screen and car is inside. And we identifies with the cars that are inside. As we outside the screen are able to control the speed, steering control, etc. of the car inside the screen. We are [[Ksetrajna]], knower or experiencer of the game. We can enjoy maximum if we identify with the driver of the car. So, this is [[false ego]]. This [[false ego]] is at the top of [[Material nature]]. In the [[game]], the road, clouds, rains, water streams, turns, slopes, etc. are all predefined. We can't change it. In real outside world we can drive expensive cars beyond our budget but in the game we can drive as expensive car as we like. This is [[illusion]]. Purpose of life = purpose of game.
