# 16 Bhāvanās with Anubhāvanās (Reference Set)

---

## 1. OBEDIENCE - ANUSARANA mālā
**Mood: “I do because I am instructed”**

1.1. O Most Merciful Sri Caitanya Mahaprabhu & Srila Prabhupada, I depend upon You alone to serve Kṛṣṇa Prasāda.
1.2. O Kṛṣṇa! I honor this prasāda because You and Your devotees have instructed me; through this obedience, I receive Your direct association.
1.3. O Prasāda Prabhu! I offer every physical act of chewing and tearing as a direct kriyā-ceṣṭā seva to You, transforming this meal into a sacred yajña.
1.4. Prasāda Prabhu is the SEVYA, I am the SEVAKA, the SEVA is repeating 'chewing and tasting that very bite'
1.5. O Śrīla Prabhupāda! By Your instruction, I engage this uncontrolled tongue in honoring Kṛṣṇa prasāda; only through Your order can I conquer it.
1.6. O Tongue! Be silent in your demands for enjoyment; you are but a servant, and your only right is to receive the spiritual taste of Kṛṣṇa's adharāmṛta.
1.7. O Tongue! You are not the enjoyer of these six rasas; you are the instrument to glorify the Lord's names and qualities through His sacred remnants.
1.8. O Kṛṣṇa! I am terrified of becoming a thief of Your gifts; I beg for the strength to honor this prasāda strictly as Your mercy, not for my own pleasure.
1.9. O Mahā-prasāda! I offer my jñāna-ceṣṭā to You by consciously perceiving Your divine flavors, seeking only the touch of Kṛṣṇa's adharāmṛta in every bite.
1.10. O Mind! Stop running here and there for palatable dishes; stay fixed on the realization that this food is non-different from Kṛṣṇa.
1.11. O Prasāda Prabhu! You are the Surya of Sat-Cit-Ananda; I immerse my tongue in Your association to burn away the darkness of material greed.
1.12. O Kṛṣṇa! I realize that if I honor You only for Your palatable taste, I fall prey to the tongue; I beg for the purity to honor You only for Your pleasure.

Golden Quote 1: The process is that you eat Prasāda, and exactly the same food you taste.

## 2. FAITH ŚRADDHA 2nd mālā

**Subtitle: PERCEIVING & PRESENCING NAMA PRABHU'S INCARNATION BEFORE ME BY FAITH**

2.1. O Nāma Prabhu! You descend as Nāma Avatara to cleanse my heart & awaken my love for You
2.2. O Nāma Prabhu! You are Kṛṣṇa Himself incarnated as Saktyavesa Sabda vigraha to give me association
2.3. O Nāma Prabhu! You are non-different from Your effulgence, the Aprākṛta Nāma & incarnation the Saktyāveśa Nāma
2.4. O Nāma Prabhu! You are Surya of infinite compassion
2.5. O Nāma Prabhu! Suddha Nāma, You are Kṛṣṇa Himself and directly pleased with chanting-cum-hearing You
2.6. O Nāma Prabhu! You are Nāma Cintamani, fulfiller of all spiritual desires

Golden Quote 2: When you say, "Hare Krishna" you try to hear the very sound, "Hare Krishna." That's all. Nothing more.

---

## 3. LOVE - PRITI 3rd mālā

**Subtitle: OFFERING FEELING of LOVE & PLEASURE-GIVING**

3.1.O Nāma Prabhu! You descending on my tongue for me is Your expression of unconditional love for me
3.2.O Nāma Prabhu! You are happy for me with every bead of my japa as father is when lost child wants to return back home
3.3.O Nāma Prabhu! May You be pleased with every bead of "pleasure-giving" by me
3.4.O Nāma Prabhu! I offer "pleasure-givings" to You with great excitement
3.5.O Nāma Prabhu! You mercifully pour all your mercy into the Saktyāvesa Nāma
3.6.O Nāma Prabhu! I practice to serve You under all & every material condition

Golden Quote 3: As you chant, try to hear each word very carefully and always complete your sixteen rounds.

---

## 4. DELIGHTFUL - HRSTAVAT 4th mālā

**Subtitle: CHANTING NAMA PRABHU DELIGHTFULLY from THE HEART**

4.1.O Nāma Prabhu! The fact of You dancing on my tongue and my cittam makes me super delighted
4.2.O Nāma Prabhu! Your omnipotent Śaktyāveṣa Nāma is source of all sat, cit and ananda mercy
4.3.O Nāma Prabhu! I offer fresh kriya ceṣṭā to chant on every bead
4.4.O Nāma Prabhu! I chant with great delight that my consciousness will soon become satcitānanda-ized
4.5.O Nāma Prabhu! I cry out to You on each bead to restore my lost Krsna consciousness
4.6.O Nāma Prabhu! Your appearance as Saktyāveṣa Nāma is the only rope of hope for humanity

Golden Quote 4: Yes. This is very important. When you will chant, you must hear also. That is wanted. Otherwise, you will be inattentive. That is offense.

---

## 5. JOYFUL-AHLĀDA 5th mālā

**Subtitle: HEARING NAMA PRABHU WITH SOUL DELIGHT, ATMA-ANANDA**

5.1.O Nāma Prabhu! conscious hearing of Your Sabda vigraha is the way soul can directly associate with You
5.2.O Nāma Prabhu! O! Hearing You is direct embracing of Your Sabda vigraha
5.3.O Nāma Prabhu! When I hear You with attention, You are dancing in my mind. This makes me joyful.
5.4.O Nāma Prabhu! You as SEVYA appear before me to receive my seva on each bead
5.5.O Nāma Prabhu! You rain down sat-cit-ānanda mercy as the Saktyavesa Vigraha for me to bathe in that rain
5.6.O Nāma Prabhu! Kṛṣṇa and all His Shakti's are contained within You. Hearing You is the way to realize Them.

Golden Quote 5: You keep your mind on the chanting. "Hare Krishna" and hear. You simply chant Hare Krishna and try to hear the sound, that's all.

---

## 6. TASKING NIRANTARA-UTSAHA 6th mālā

**Subtitle: TASKING TO BUILD UP PLEASURE-GIVINGS to NAMA PRABHU**

6.1.O Nāma Prabhu! You are very pleased with uninterrupted and undiverted attention on You.
6.2.O Nāma Prabhu! I offer atma kārya ceṣṭā with greed to offer more and more pleasure-givings
6.3.O Nāma Prabhu! bless me to stay immersed in Your effulgence by zero-attention break japa
6.4.O Nāma Prabhu! I offer every additional bead of pleasure-giving with 'success' and 'accomplishment' rasa
6.5.O Nāma Prabhu! O! Surya of Sat-Cit-Ananda, I am greedy to please You more and more
6.6.O Nāma Prabhu! piling up present hearing over past hearing without thinking accelerates flow of sat-cit-ananda energy

Golden Quote 6: When you chant, you must hear also. This is attention. The chanting must be heard by you. That is attention.

---

## 7. RELATIONSHIP-SAMBANDHA 7th mālā

**Subtitle: MY RELATIONSHIP WITH NAMA PRABHU as NAMA PREMI SEVAK**

7.1.O Nāma Prabhu! I offer relationship of surrender on every bead
7.2.O Nāma Prabhu! I am Your Nama Premi with desire to please You on every bead
7.3.O Nāma Prabhu! I culture a japa doer-personality and link hearing to that very chant
7.4.O Nāma Prabhu! I like to practice offering self-surrender for Your pleasure
7.5.O Nāma Prabhu! I try my best to meet Your expectation from me on each bead
7.6.O Nāma Prabhu! I beg for mercy to serve You purely, free from any offenses.

Golden Quote 7: While chanting, you must hear. You must give attention to hear.

---

## 8. SURRENDER - SARANAGATI 8th mālā

**Subtitle: SURRENDERING THE SELF & LIFE to NAMA PRABHU**

8.1.O Nāma Prabhu! I surrender unto You. I am Yours, do what You want to do with me.
8.2.O Nāma Prabhu! I offer 'nirmama bhavana' - nothing is mine.
8.3.O Nāma Prabhu! Give me spiritual strength to act only for Your plans and pleasure
8.4.O Nāma Prabhu! I beg You for realization - I am Yours and You alone are there for me
8.5.O Nāma Prabhu! everything in existe moves as per your Your plans & pleasures.
8.6.O Nāma Prabhu! You are Supremely Pure. Your association makes me purified.

Golden Quote 8: The words "Hare Krishna" should be very distinctly pronounced & heard. Chanting is very simple, but one must practice it seriously.

---

## 9. MAINTAINED - BHARTĀPĀTRA 9th mālā

**Subtitle: RECOGNIZING THAT NAMA PRABHU is MY ONLY MAINTAINER**

9.1.O Nāma Prabhu! You are direct master, maintainer & enjoyer of your devotees.
9.2.O Nāma Prabhu! You declare that You are suhrdaṁ sarva-bhūtānām
9.3.O Nāma Prabhu! You are 'within and without' everything.
9.4.O Nāma Prabhu! You, as Lord in the heart are provider and sustainer -yoga-kşemam vahāmy aham
9.5.O Nāma Prabhu! You, as Caitanya Mahaprabhu, are heading and leading Your divine preaching mission
9.6.O Nāma Prabhu! You are bhaktavatsala. You take charge of Your devotee's life with great care.

Golden Quote 9: Therefore, to clear this cloudy consciousness Chaitanya Mahaprabhu recommended that one should simply chant the holy name of God sincerely and hear it with attention.

---

## 10. PROTECTED - RAKSAPĀTRA 10th mālā

**Subtitle: RECOGNIZING THAT NAMA PRABHU is MY ONLY PROTECTOR**

10.1.O Nāma Prabhu! You always protect Your devotees as bhaktarakshaka
10.2.O Nāma Prabhu! wherever Your Sat-Cit-Ananda effulgence shines, the darkness of Māyā vanishes
10.3.O Nāma Prabhu! You are bhaktavatsala
10.4.O Nāma Prabhu! You are destroyer of all demons & demoniac forces: asura kula-nāśana
10.5.O Nāma Prabhu! You send tremors of fear into the hearts of demons
10.6.O Nāma Prabhu! O! Please protect me from two arrows of MAYA - cover my pure consciousness and bewilder my perception.

Golden Quote 10: Yes. Chanting and hearing, locked up. Mind is locked up. And because Krishna vibration is locked up, then Krishna is locked up.

---

## 11. HUMILITY-VINAMRTĀ yogi 11th mālā

**Subtitle: CHANT WITH HUMILITY BEFORE NAMA PRABHU**

11.1.O Nāma Prabhu! You are the Lord of all Yoga-māyā and Maha-māyā
11.2.O Nāma Prabhu! Those who are servant of Your dear servants endear themselves to You easily
11.3.O Nāma Prabhu! You are the only source and shelter of all jivas
11.4.O Nāma Prabhu! When You destroy all kinds of false pride in Your devotees. That is Your special mercy.
11.5.O Nāma Prabhu! I am solely made up of Your Sat-Cit-Ananda prasādam. It is due to the mercy of Srila Prabhupada.
11.6.O Nāma Prabhu! Hearing You alone can make me free from desire for name, fame, adoration etc.

Golden Quote 11: As soon as you chant Hare Krishna, you hear it, and then your mind becomes locked up.

---

## 12. ETERNAL SHELTER - CARANĀŚRAYA 12th mālā

**Subtitle: BEGGING TOTAL SHELTER EXISTENTIALLY & SITUATIONALLY**

12.1.O Nāma Prabhu! You are the only way to attain the Lotus feet of Krishna
12.2.O Nāma Prabhu! Please make me feel Your Limitless Mightiness and my Tiny-ness
12.3.O Nāma Prabhu! You will never forsake Your devotees who have taken shelter of Your lotus feet
12.4.O Nāma Prabhu! Please make me feel Your limitless kindness & sweetness
12.5.O Nāma Prabhu! Remembrance of your Lotus feet dissolves and dissipates all 'false-egos' from the cittam.
12.6.O Nāma Prabhu! You alone can awaken my buddha citta and pure Krishna consciousness

Golden quote 12: By chanting Hare Krishna, one captures the mind immediately. Just by saying the name Krishna and hearing it, the mind is automatically fixed on Krishna.

---

## 13. BEGGING MERCY - KRIPA PEKSHA 13th mālā

**Subtitle: BEGGING FOR CAUSELESS SAT, CIT, ANANDA MERCY**

13.1.O Nāma Prabhu! You are infinite pure consciousness who can awaken my Suddha citta
13.2.O Nāma Prabhu! You are source of all knowledge-remembrance-forgetfullness-seated in everyone's heart
13.3.O Nāma Prabhu! I beg You to infuse in me Your ajna (order) for me. May my life be lead, inspired and directed by You.
13.4.O Nāma Prabhu! I beg You to engage me in Your service and liberate me from Maya's service.
13.5.O Nāma Prabhu! I beg you for vairagya-virakti (detest, disgust of kama sankalpa) shakti to be fixed up in Your divine service
13.6.O Nāma Prabhu! I beg You to gift me divine taste of Your all-attractive namas, rupas, gunas, lilas and dhamas.

Diamond Quote: You have to chant and hear. That's all. You have to chant with your tongue and the same sound you hear. That's all.

## 14. GRATITUDE-KRTAJÑĀRPANAM 14th mālā

**Subtitle: OFFERING GRATITUDE to NAMA PRABHU**

14.1.O Nāma Prabhu! I am eternally grateful for descending as the Surya of Sat-Cit-Ananda on my tongue and mind.
14.2.O Nāma Prabhu! You are giver and taker of everything from Your devotees for their good
14.3.O Nāma Prabhu! My gratitude for Your devotional service of Nama Seva which alone can free me from Māyā.
14.4.O Nāma Prabhu! My gratitude for the Absolute true perspective of the world through spiritual knowledge
14.5.O Nāma Prabhu! My gratitude to You for exposing the grand illusion of material life.
14.6.O Nāma Prabhu! Your association by hearing You transforms my animal consciousness to divine peacefulness

Diamond Quote: This is the only method, that you chant loudly and hear Hare Krsna. If your mind is in other things, it will be forced to concentrate on the sound vibration "Kṛṣṇa."

---

## 15. FORGIVENESS - KSAMĀPEKSHA 15th mālā

**Subtitle: BEGGING FORGIVENESS from NAMA PRABHU**

15.1.O Nāma Prabhu! Forgive me neglecting Your presence before me as the Supreme Personality of entire existence.
15.2.O Nāma Prabhu! Please forgive me for misunderstanding You & Your unconditional love.
15.3.O Nāma Prabhu! Please forgive me for forgetting You. Bless me to get back my eternal life with You.
15.4.O Nāma Prabhu! You are the embodiment of Krsna's compassion Who forgives all offenses.
15.5.O Nāma Prabhu! acknowledging Your forgiving nature I shall continuously strive not to make offenses in future.
15.6.O Nāma Prabhu! I realize that only offenses to You and Your dear devotees stop us from nectar of Your association.

Diamond Quote: If mind is so agitated, you cannot concentrate. But if you chant loudly, "Hare Krishna!" your mind will be forced to be drawn and hear Krishna.

---

## 16. BEGGING SERVICE - SEVĀPEKSHA 16th mālā

**Subtitle: BEGGING FOR SERVICE from NAMA PRABHU**

16.1.O Nāma Prabhu! only devotional service is eternal. Actions of false ego imprison the soul to karma and illusion
16.2.O! Most Merciful Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu & Srila Prabhupada, I beg you to bestow upon me service in Your mission.
16.3.O Nāma Prabhu! I shall practice surrendering all my works unto You.
16.4.O! Most Merciful Srila Prabhupada, kindly manifest Your orders into my mind for serving Your mission.
16.5.O Nāma Prabhu! I take both direction anxiety and resource anxiety work to fulfill your mission with faith in the help from You as Supersoul.
16.6.O! Dear Srila Prabhupada, Please gift me with Nāma Prachārak servant-ego and use me to further Your mission.

Diamond Quote: Do not be disappointed if sometimes your mind gives some agitation. Chant loudly whenever there is disturbance and hear the transcendental sound attentively.
